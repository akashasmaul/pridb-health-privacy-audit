# Research roadmap

PriDB-Health is a working baseline for a larger privacy-engineering research program.

## Near term

- sensitivity analysis for PRS weights and normalization;
- membership, linkage, and attribute-inference attack benchmarks;
- Linux/macOS PostgreSQL automation and containerized reproducibility;
- confidence intervals across datasets and random seeds;
- formal DP accounting across repeated queries.

## Medium term

- longitudinal and rare-disease cohort utility studies;
- adaptive generalization selected by constrained optimization;
- multi-hospital and federated evaluation;
- workload-aware privacy budgets and query auditing;
- expert review of GDPR/HIPAA control mappings.

## Long term

- validated public-health or clinical research deployments;
- human-centered studies with data stewards and researchers;
- formal threat models and machine-checkable policy guarantees;
- independent replication across countries and health systems.

Progress should be measured through transparent preregistered experiments, not only higher headline scores.
